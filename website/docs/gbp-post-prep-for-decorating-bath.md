# GBP post: Importance of prep for decorating in the Bath area

Use this when posting about **preparation for decorating** on **Google Business Profile** (Google Business → Posts → Create post). Highlights why prep matters — especially in the Bath area, where period properties and older surfaces need careful preparation.

---

## 1. Image (optional but recommended)

- **Option A:** Photo of prep in progress — filling, sanding, masking, or a wall ready for paint. Resize to **1080 × 1080 px** (square) or **1080 × 1350 px** (portrait). Minimum width **720 px**.
- **Option B:** Before/after showing a surface before and after prep (or before prep vs finished paint).
- **Option C:** Your logo or a simple graphic with "Prep matters – Bath & BANES" or "Preparation for decorating".

Avoid stock images that don't reflect your actual work.

---

## 2. Post type

- **Update** (standard post) – good for advice and expertise.

---

## 3. Copy for the post

**Headline** (keep under ~58 characters):

```
Why prep matters for decorating in the Bath area
```

**Alternative headlines:**

```
Preparation – the key to a great finish in Bath
```

```
Prep for decorating in Bath – why it matters
```

**Description** (paste into the post text):

```
Preparation is the most important part of decorating — especially in the Bath area. Many Bath homes are period properties with lime plaster, older woodwork and character surfaces. They need proper filling, sanding and priming before paint, or the finish won't last.

I've been decorating in Bath and BANES for over 25 years. I don't skip the prep — and neither should you. Free quote for your next job.
```

**Shorter option** (if character limit is tight):

```
Prep matters for decorating in Bath — period homes need proper filling, sanding and priming. 25+ years in the area. Free quote.
```

## 5. In Google Business Profile

1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com) and open your **New Decorating** profile.
2. Click **Posts** (or **Updates** / **Add update**).
3. **Create post** → choose **Update**.
4. **Add photo** → upload your image (prep in progress, before/after, or graphic).
5. **Headline** → paste one of the headlines above.
6. **Description** → paste the description (or shorter version).
7. **Button** → set label (e.g. "Get a quote" or "Learn more") and URL: `https://new-decorating.co.uk/contact` or your prep blog post URL.
8. **Publish**.
