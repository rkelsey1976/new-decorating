### Target word count (homepage)
- **Current**: Relatively concise; hero + trust bar + services + areas + reviews + FAQ + blog teasers.

### Featured snippet opportunity
- **FAQ section**: Yes. Format each question as an H2 or H3 with a 40–60 word answer immediately after (e.g. "How do I get a quote?", "What areas do you cover?"). Current FAQ is strong; ensure answers are concise and directly under the question heading.

## 2. Competitive Landscape

### Primary competitor: BPD Bath Painters & Decorators (bathpainters.co.uk)

**Website**: https://www.bathpainters.co.uk/

BPD is the main competitor. Their domain and brand name ("Bath Painters & Decorators") align closely with the primary keyword "painter decorator Bath."

#### BPD homepage at a glance
- **Headline**: "Professional Painting & Decorating Services in Bath" (title: "Professional Painting & Decorating Services in Bath | Bath Painters & Decorators").
- **Subline**: "Professional painting and decorating services for residential and commercial properties in Bath and surrounding areas."
- **Banner**: "Now serving Bristol & Clifton — find out more →" (expansion into Bristol).
- **Services** (7): Residential, Commercial, Spraying, Sash Window Refurbishment, Plastering, Wallpaper, Full Fit Outs — each links to a service page.
- **Our Work**: Named project case studies (e.g. Chewton House Bath, Grand Palais Paris for Dolce & Gabbana, Little Orchard Bath); "View All Projects" → portfolio.
- **Instagram**: @bathpainters.
- **Video**: "See Us in Action" — e.g. Grand Palais project.
- **Google Reviews**: "5.0 rating from our happy customers" with 3 excerpts; "See all reviews on Google →".
- **CTA**: "Book Free Quote" (→ /book-quote) and "Call 01225 690 039" (click-to-call).

#### BPD About (bathpainters.co.uk/about)
- Family business founded **2021** by Dennis and Matt; Joe in management. "Bath's trusted painting and decorating specialists."
- **17 full-time decorators.** Part of BPD Group Holdings (BPD Joinery, The Handymen Who Can).
- **Why Choose BPD**: Quality Assured, Reliable Service, Expert Team.
- **No FAQ** on homepage.

#### BPD strengths (vs New Decorating)
- Domain/brand contains "Bath Painters" (keyword-rich).
- **5.0 rating** stated on homepage.
- **Portfolio** with named projects and high-profile work.
- **Video** section.
- **Wider services**: spraying, sash windows, plastering, fit outs.
- Dual CTA: Book quote + click-to-call.
- **Instagram** for social proof.

#### BPD weaknesses / New Decorating opportunities
- **Experience**: BPD founded 2021 vs **New Decorating 25+ years** — lead with longevity.
- **Geography**: BPD "Bath and surrounding areas" + Bristol; **New Decorating** names Keynsham, Midsomer Norton, Radstock, BANES and has **area pages + local blog** — stronger for Keynsham/BANES.
- **No FAQ** on BPD — **New Decorating's FAQ** is a differentiator and snippet opportunity.
- **Personal vs team**: BPD = 17 decorators; **New Decorating** = one decorator (Jay) — single point of contact.
- **Insurance**: New Decorating "Fully insured"; BPD doesn't emphasise it on homepage.
- **Small jobs**: New Decorating FAQ "Do you do small jobs? Yes"; BPD doesn't spell this out.

### Other competitor homepages (secondary)

#### Flawless Finish (flawlessfinishdecorators.co.uk)
- **Positioning**: "High-calibre" painters & decorators, interior design, refurbishment, maintenance.
- **Homepage**: Repeated hero with "REQUEST A QUOTE"; services (Residential, Exterior, Interior, Interior Design, UPVC, Carpentry); "What our customers say" with 7 testimonials and star ratings; quote form on page; long FAQ; area list (Bath neighbourhoods).
- **Strengths**: Multiple CTAs, quote form on homepage, many testimonials with locations, FAQ, clear phone/email.
- **Weaknesses**: Repetitive hero blocks; very long page.

#### RK Decorating Services (rkdecoratingservices.co.uk)
- **Positioning**: "Professional qualified painter and decorator based in Bath", 25 years.
- **Homepage**: Single hero; "Call Rich" CTA; three services (Interior, Exterior, Wallpaper) with "Learn More"; testimonials (3); "Why Choose US" (Satisfaction Guarantee, One-Stop Shop, Free Consultation); contact form and phone.
- **Strengths**: Clear one-person brand, simple structure, phone-first CTA.
- **Weaknesses**: Less local keyword variation; no FAQ on homepage.

#### New Decorating (current) — strengths and gaps
- **Strengths**: Clear value bar (25+ years, insured, free quotes, local, tidy); good service and area coverage; real Google reviews; FAQ; blog with local area content (Widcombe, Odd Down, Weston); internal links to area pages.
- **Gaps vs competitors**: No star rating or review count in hero/trust bar; no before/after or project image in hero; phone number could be more prominent as click-to-call; meta could include Keynsham.

## 3. Trust Signals & Conversion (research-backed)

Findings from contractor/trade website research:

| Element | Impact | New Decorating status |
|--------|--------|------------------------|
| Reviews & ratings | +34% conversion | Has Google reviews; add star rating + count to hero/trust bar |
| Before/after photos | +23% | Gallery exists; add one strong image to hero or first section |
| Real team/owner photo | +19% | Consider "About" or hero photo of Jay |
| Years in business | +12% | ✅ 25+ years in trust bar |
| Insurance | +8% | ✅ "Fully insured"; optional: short line or badge |
| Clear phone/contact | Foundation | ✅ Phone in header; ensure click-to-call and visibility on mobile |
| Fast load, mobile-first | Foundation | Verify; most local searches are mobile |

Recommendations:
- Add **Google star rating and review count** (e.g. "Rated 5 stars on Google") in or just below the hero.
- Add **one hero image** (recent project or before/after) to support "professional" and "flawless finish".
- Keep **phone (07717 772881)** prominent; add a secondary CTA such as "Call for a free quote" beside "Get A Free Quote".
- Optional: Short **insurance line** ("Fully insured for your peace of mind") if not already clear.

## 6. Meta & On-Page SEO

### Meta title (current)
`Painter & Decorator Bath | Interior & Exterior Painting | New Decorating`  
- **Length**: Within 50–60 chars ideal; current is fine.
- **Improvement**: Consider adding Keynsham if space: e.g. `Painter & Decorator Bath & Keynsham | New Decorating` (or keep as is and use Keynsham in description).

### Meta description (draft)
- **Current** (from page): Professional painting and decorating across Bath and North East Somerset. 25+ years experience. Free quotes.
- **Enhanced** (150–160 chars): "Professional painter & decorator in Bath, Keynsham & BANES. Interior & exterior, wallpaper. 25+ years, fully insured. Free quote. Get in touch."
- **Alternative**: "Painter & decorator for Bath, Keynsham, Midsomer Norton & BANES. Interior, exterior & wallpaper. 25+ years. Free quotes. Contact for a no-obligation quote."

### URL
- Homepage: `/` — no change.

### Critical keyword placement
- **H1**: "Painter & Decorator for Your Properties in Bath" — keep; primary keyword present.
- **First 100 words**: Include "painter," "decorator," "Bath," "painting and decorating," "North East Somerset" or "BANES."
- **Subheads**: Use "interior painting," "exterior painting," "areas I cover," "free quote" where natural.
- **Conclusion/CTA**: Reuse "painter and decorator," "Bath," "Keynsham," "free quote."

## 7. Differentiation Strategy

- **Primary competitor**: BPD Bath Painters (bathpainters.co.uk). They have a keyword-rich domain, 5.0 rating on homepage, portfolio with named projects, video, and wider services (spraying, sash, plastering). New Decorating should emphasise **25+ years' experience** (BPD founded 2021), **one trusted decorator** (vs 17 staff), **explicit BANES/Keynsham/Midsomer Norton coverage** with area pages and local blog, **FAQ** (BPD has none), and **"Fully insured"** in the trust bar.
- **Vs Flawless Finish**: Fewer services but clearer focus (painting & decorating only); simpler message; strong FAQ and blog for local SEO.
- **Vs RK**: Similar one-expert positioning; New Decorating leads on Keynsham/BANES coverage, FAQ, and blog.
- **Unique angles**: 25+ years, "Clean & tidy," "free quotes," "Do you do small jobs? Yes," area-specific blog (Widcombe, Odd Down, Weston), full BANES coverage. Add **star rating + review count** on homepage to match BPD's trust signal.
