## Quick wins for your homepage

1. **Add a “Call” button** in the final CTA next to “Contact us” (e.g. “Call 07717 772881”).
2. **Mention Google reviews** near testimonials: e.g. “Rated 5 on Google” + “See our Google reviews” (or your real rating).
3. **Optional: one “Featured project”** block – one image, 2–3 lines, link to gallery – plus a job count if you’re happy with the number.
4. **Optional: “Book a free quote” or “Get a free quote”** as a sticky or nav CTA linking to `/contact`.
5. **Optional: fourth service card** (e.g. “Preparation & repair”) or a short “Also: …” line with links to other services.
