# GBP post: Online estimates – use our form and we’ll get back to you

Use this when posting about **online estimates** and your **contact form** on **Google Business Profile** (Google Business → Posts → Create post). Encourages people to submit their details so you can get back to them with a quote.

---

## 1. Image (optional but recommended)

- **Option A:** Your logo — simple and recognisable.
- **Option B:** A photo of you at work or a finished job (shows you’re real and busy).
- **Option C:** A simple graphic with “Free quote” or “Get an estimate” and your website/contact line.
- Resize to **1080 × 1080 px** (square) or **1080 × 1350 px** (portrait). Minimum width **720 px**.

---

## 2. Post type

- **Update** (standard post) – best for “get a quote” / “use our form” messaging.

---

## 3. Copy for the post

**Headline** (keep under ~58 characters):

```
Get an online estimate — use our form, we’ll get back to you
```

**Alternative headlines:**

```
Free quote: fill in our form and we’ll get back to you
```

```
Online estimates — tell us what you need, we’ll reply
```

**Description** (paste into the post text):

```
Need a quote for painting and decorating? Use our online form and we’ll get back to you. Tell us roughly what you need — a room, a full redecoration, exterior work — and your location, and we’ll come back with a free, no-obligation estimate.

No need to chase: we reply to every enquiry. Bath, Keynsham, Midsomer Norton, Radstock and across BANES. 25+ years’ experience.
```

**Shorter option** (if character limit is tight):

```
Get a free quote: use our form on the website and we’ll get back to you. Painting and decorating across Bath and BANES. No obligation.
```

---

## 4. Call-to-action button

- **Button text:** `Get an estimate` or `Request a quote` or `Contact us`
- **Link:** `https://new-decorating.co.uk/contact`

This takes people straight to your contact page where the form lives.

(Add in GBP when it asks for “Button” or “Call to action”.)

---

## 5. In Google Business Profile

1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com) and open your **New Decorating** profile.
2. Click **Posts** (or **Updates** / **Add update**).
3. **Create post** → choose **Update**.
4. **Add photo** → upload your image (logo or job photo).
5. **Headline** → paste one of the headlines above.
6. **Description** → paste the description (or shorter version).
7. **Button** → set label (e.g. “Get an estimate”) and URL: `https://new-decorating.co.uk/contact`.
8. **Publish**.

---

## 6. Why this helps

- **Clear next step:** People know to use the form and that you’ll respond.
- **Low friction:** “Use our form” is easier than “call or email” for many.
- **Sets expectation:** “We’ll get back to you” reduces uncertainty and builds trust.
