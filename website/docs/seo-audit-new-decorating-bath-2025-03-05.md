## 2. Top SEO Improvements

### 2.1 Meta description (add or refine)

- **Issue**: No visible meta description in the snippet; Google may pull generic text.
- **Action**: Add a unique meta description, 150–160 characters, with primary keyword + CTA.
- **Example**:  
  `Professional painter & decorator in Bath. Interior, exterior, period properties. 25+ years experience, free quote. Call 07717 772881 or get a quote.`

### 2.2 Front-load keywords in first 100 words

- **Issue**: Intent is clear but you can strengthen relevance for "painter Bath", "decorator Bath", "painting and decorating Bath" in the opening.
- **Action**: In the first 100 words, include at least:
  - "painter" or "painter and decorator"
  - "Bath"
  - "painting and decorating"
  - One of: "interior", "exterior", "quote"
- **Example opening line**:  
  `Need a painter and decorator in Bath? I offer professional painting and decorating across Bath — from the city centre to Combe Down, Larkhall, Widcombe and the surrounding villages.`

### 2.3 Add an FAQ section (featured snippet opportunity)

- **Issue**: No FAQ; you're missing a strong chance for FAQ rich results and "People also ask" visibility.
- **Action**: Add one H2 (e.g. "Frequently asked questions about painting and decorating in Bath") and 4–6 H3 questions with 40–80 word answers. Use natural language people search for.
- **Suggested questions**:
  - How much does a painter and decorator cost in Bath?
  - Do you cover [specific area]? (e.g. Combe Down, Larkhall)
  - Are you insured for work in Bath?
  - Do you do exterior painting on Bath stone?
  - How do I get a quote for painting and decorating in Bath?
  - Do you work on period and Georgian properties in Bath?

### 2.4 Lengthen and structure content (without fluff)

- **Issue**: Page is relatively short; competitors often have 900–1,500+ words on area pages.
- **Action**:
  - Keep "Painting & decorating in Bath" and "Local areas I cover" but avoid listing the same areas twice (merge "Local areas" and "Nearby areas" or make one "Bath neighbourhoods" and one "Nearby towns").
  - Add a short "Why choose a local painter and decorator in Bath" (3–4 bullets: 25+ years, period property experience, free quote, fully insured).
  - Add the FAQ section (see above).
  - Add 1–2 sentences per key neighbourhood if you can (e.g. "Combe Down and Bathwick have many period and stone properties — I'm used to working with lime and traditional finishes.").

### 2.5 Internal linking

- **Issue**: Service links exist ("Learn more about each service") but could be more strategic for SEO and UX.
- **Action**:
  - Link to main service pages with keyword-rich anchors, e.g. "interior painting in Bath", "exterior painting", "wallpaper hanging", "preparation and repair".
  - Add one contextual link to the main "Areas" hub or homepage where it fits (e.g. "See all areas I cover").
  - Ensure "All areas →" and any area list link to the correct area URLs.

### 2.6 Trust signals above the fold

- **Issue**: "25+ years experience" and "Fully insured" appear in the footer; they're strong for both SEO and conversion.
- **Action**: In the hero or first paragraph, add a short trust line, e.g.  
  `25+ years experience. Fully insured. Free, no-obligation quote.`  
  So visitors see credibility immediately.

### 2.7 Meta title tweak (optional, for CTR)

- **Current**: Painters in Bath \| Painter & Decorator \| New Decorating  
- **Alternative**: Painter & Decorator Bath \| Free Quote \| 25+ Years \| New Decorating  
  (Keep under 60 characters; test for click-through rate.)

---

## 3. Technical / Schema

- **LocalBusiness schema**: Ensure the page (or site-wide) has LocalBusiness JSON-LD with name, area served (Bath), phone, address if applicable, and optionally "areaServed" covering Bath and listed villages.
- **FAQPage schema**: If you add the FAQ section, add FAQPage schema so Google can show FAQ rich results.
- **Breadcrumbs**: Breadcrumb list is present; ensure it's marked up with BreadcrumbList schema if not already.
