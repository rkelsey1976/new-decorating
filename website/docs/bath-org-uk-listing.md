New Decorating is a professional painting and decorating business based in Bath, serving homeowners across Bath and North East Somerset (BANES), Wiltshire and Somerset.

I offer interior and exterior painting, wallpaper hanging, and preparation & repair — from single rooms to full house redecorations, period properties and new builds. I focus on quality finishes, thorough preparation and working cleanly with minimal disruption. All work is quoted upfront with free, no-obligation quotes. I'm fully insured and have over 25 years of experience.

Areas covered: Bath, Keynsham, Midsomer Norton, Radstock, Trowbridge, Chippenham, Rode, Melksham, Bradford on Avon, Frome and surrounding villages.

Get in touch via the website or phone for a free quote.
