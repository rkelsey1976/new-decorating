# GBP post: Painters in Bath

Use this when posting on **Google Business Profile** to target the search **"Painters in Bath"**. Many people search "painters" (plural) rather than "painter and decorator" — this post reinforces that phrase on your GBP so Google associates you with that query.

---

## 1. Image (optional but recommended)

- **Option A:** Photo of a job in Bath (interior, exterior, or finished room). **1080 × 1080 px** or **1080 × 1350 px**. Min width **720 px**.
- **Option B:** Logo or graphic with **"Painters in Bath"** and your name/contact.
- **Option C:** Local job photo from Bath. Avoid stock images.

---

## 2. Post type

- **Update** (standard post).

---

## 3. Copy for the post

**Headline** (under ~58 characters):

```
Painters in Bath — painter & decorator, 25+ years
```

**Alternative headline:**

```
Painters in Bath | Interior, exterior, wallpaper
```

**Description** (paste into post text):

```
Painters in Bath — I'm a painter and decorator working across Bath and BANES. Interior and exterior painting, wallpaper, preparation and repair. 25+ years, free quotes. Get in touch to confirm I cover your area.
```

**Shorter option:**

```
Painters in Bath. Painter & decorator — interior, exterior, wallpaper. 25+ years. Free quotes.
```

---

## 4. Call-to-action button

- **Button:** `Get a quote` or `Free quote` or `Visit site`
- **Link:** `https://new-decorating.co.uk/contact` or `https://new-decorating.co.uk`

---

## 5. In Google Business Profile

1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com) → your **New Decorating** profile.
2. **Posts** → **Create post** → **Update**.
3. Add photo (job in Bath, or "Painters in Bath" graphic).
4. **Headline:** e.g. "Painters in Bath — painter & decorator, 25+ years".
5. **Description:** paste the description above (or shorter version).
6. **Button:** "Get a quote" → `https://new-decorating.co.uk/contact`.
7. **Publish**.

---

## 6. Why this helps

- **"Painters in Bath"** is a common search; "painter and decorator" often ranks instead. Using "Painters in Bath" in GBP posts (and in your **GBP business description** if you can) helps Google show you for that query.
- Your **website** now uses "Painters in Bath" in the main title and meta — so GBP posts + site together reinforce the phrase.

**Tip:** In GBP → **Edit profile** → **Business description**, include "Painters in Bath" or "We are painters in Bath" naturally (e.g. "Painters in Bath. Professional painter and decorator across Bath and BANES. Interior, exterior, wallpaper. 25+ years. Free quotes.") so your listing itself targets the phrase.
