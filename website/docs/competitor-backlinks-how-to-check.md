# How to check competitor backlinks (Bath Painters & you)

You can’t see a full backlink profile without using an SEO tool. Here’s how to check **Bath Painters** (bathpainters.co.uk) and compare with **New Decorating** (new-decorating.co.uk), plus what to do with the results.

---

## 1. Free tools you can use

| Tool | What you get (free) | URL |
|------|----------------------|-----|
| **Ahrefs Backlink Checker** | Backlinks, referring domains, Domain Rating (DR), sample of linking pages | [ahrefs.com/backlink-checker](https://ahrefs.com/backlink-checker) |
| **Semrush Backlink Checker** | Top backlinks, Authority Score, referring domains (limited without sign-up) | [semrush.com](https://www.semrush.com) → Backlink Analytics |
| **Moz Link Explorer** | ~10 free queries/month: Domain Authority (DA), linking domains, top links | [moz.com/link-explorer](https://moz.com/link-explorer) |
| **OpenLinkProfiler** | Backlinks, domain rank, dofollow/nofollow, anchor text | [openlinkprofiler.org](https://openlinkprofiler.org/) |

Use **one** of these and run the same check for both domains so the numbers are comparable.

---

## 2. What to check for Bath Painters (bathpainters.co.uk)

1. **Domain-level metrics**
   - **Domain Rating (Ahrefs)** or **Domain Authority (Moz)** or **Authority Score (Semrush)** – strength of their link profile.
   - **Referring domains** – how many different sites link to them.
   - **Total backlinks** – total number of links (less important than referring domains).

2. **Where the links come from**
   - **Directory listings** – Yell, FreeIndex, Thomson Local, Bath.org, Checkatrade, Trustpilot, etc.
   - **Local / Bath** – bath.org.uk, Visit Bath, local business lists, “best painters Bath” roundups.
   - **Trade / trade bodies** – trade associations, insurance/supplier pages.
   - **Blogs / press** – local news, home improvement blogs, case studies.
   - **Social** – Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn (usually nofollow; still good for discovery).

3. **Anchor text**
   - Do they get “Bath painters”, “painter decorator Bath”, “Bath Painters and Decorators”, etc.? That supports local and brand queries.

4. **Quality vs quantity**
   - A few strong links (real directories, local sites, one good editorial) beat lots of spam or irrelevant links. Note any that look like paid/spam and ignore those when comparing.

---

## 3. Compare with your site (new-decorating.co.uk)

Run the **same tool** for **new-decorating.co.uk** and note:

- **Your DR/DA/Authority Score** vs theirs.
- **Your referring domains** vs theirs.
- **Types of links** they have that you don’t (e.g. specific directories, Bath.org, roundups).

Then you can target similar places (directories, local lists, trade) for your own backlinks.

---

## 4. What we already know (from earlier research)

- **bath.org.uk** – Bath-related directory; Bath Painters appears in “Painters And Decorators in Bath City Centre”. You should try to get listed there too if it’s a proper business directory.
- **Social** – They have Facebook and Instagram in schema; links from those are typically nofollow but still part of their “footprint”.
- **Reviews** – Google Business Profile and review sites (e.g. Trustpilot, Checkatrade) often link to the site; those count as backlinks and support local SEO.

So when you run the backlink checker, look for: **bath.org.uk**, **Yell**, **FreeIndex**, **Checkatrade**, **Trustpilot**, **Thomson Local**, **Facebook**, **Instagram**, and any “best painters Bath” or local roundups.

## 5. Quick step-by-step (e.g. Ahrefs free)

1. Go to [ahrefs.com/backlink-checker](https://ahrefs.com/backlink-checker).
2. Enter **bathpainters.co.uk** → note **Domain Rating**, **Referring domains**, **Backlinks**.
3. Open the list of **referring domains** or **top backlinks** and note the **types of sites** (directories, local, trade, blogs).
4. Repeat for **new-decorating.co.uk**.
5. List **gaps**: e.g. “They have Yell, Checkatrade, bath.org.uk; we only have …”.
6. Add those gaps to your **citation/listings plan** (see `business-directories-checklist.md`, `seo-keynsham.md`) and prioritise the ones that are relevant and realistic (same NAP everywhere).
