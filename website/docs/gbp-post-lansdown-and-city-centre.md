# GBP post: Lansdown and Bath city centre – painter & decorator

Use this when posting about your coverage in **Lansdown** and the **Bath city centre** on **Google Business Profile** (Google Business → Posts → Create post). Targets both areas in one post for "painter decorator Lansdown", "painter Bath city centre" and similar searches.

---

## 1. Image (optional but recommended)

- **Option A:** Photo of a job in Lansdown or the city centre (e.g. period property, Georgian terrace, finished room). Resize to **1080 × 1080 px** (square) or **1080 × 1350 px** (portrait). Minimum width **720 px**.
- **Option B:** Your logo or a simple graphic with "Lansdown & Bath city centre" and "Painter & decorator".
- **Option C:** A local job photo from Lansdown or central Bath. Avoid stock images that don't reflect your work.

---

## 2. Post type

- **Update** (standard post) – best for "areas we cover" and local visibility.

---

## 3. Copy for the post

**Headline** (keep under ~58 characters):

```
Lansdown & Bath city centre – painter & decorator
```

**Alternative headlines:**

```
Painter & decorator in Lansdown and Bath city centre
```

```
Lansdown, Bath city centre – 25+ years, free quotes
```

**Description** (paste into the post text):

```
I'm a painter and decorator covering Lansdown and the Bath city centre. Whether you're on Lansdown Road or in the centre of Bath — period townhouses, Georgian terraces, flats and character homes — I've been working in both areas for over 25 years.

Interior and exterior painting, wallpaper, preparation and repair. Bath stone, period features and modern finishes. Every job is quoted individually with a free, no-obligation quote. Get in touch to confirm I cover your street.
```

**Shorter option** (if character limit is tight):

```
Painter and decorator in Lansdown and Bath city centre. Interior, exterior, wallpaper. 25+ years. Free quotes.
```

---

## 4. Call-to-action button

- **Button text:** `Get a quote` or `Free quote` or `View areas`
- **Link (Bath area page):** `https://new-decorating.co.uk/area/bath`
- **Link (Lansdown area page):** `https://new-decorating.co.uk/area/lansdown`
- **Link (contact):** `https://new-decorating.co.uk/contact`

Use **area/bath** to send people to the full Bath areas list (includes city centre and Lansdown); use **contact** for a direct quote CTA.

(Add in GBP when it asks for "Button" or "Call to action".)

---

## 5. In Google Business Profile

1. Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com) and open your **New Decorating** profile.
2. Click **Posts** (or **Updates** / **Add update**).
3. **Create post** → choose **Update**.
4. **Add photo** → upload your image (job in Lansdown or city centre, logo, or area graphic).
5. **Headline** → paste one of the headlines above.
6. **Description** → paste the description (or shorter version).
7. **Button** → set label (e.g. "Get a quote" or "View areas") and URL: `https://new-decorating.co.uk/area/bath` or `https://new-decorating.co.uk/contact`.
8. **Publish**.

---

## 6. Why this helps

- **Local SEO:** Reinforces "Lansdown" and "Bath city centre" on your GBP so Google associates you with both for relevant searches.
- **Clarity:** Shows you work in north Bath (Lansdown) and the city centre in one post.
- **Consistency:** Matches your website's Bath and Lansdown area pages.
